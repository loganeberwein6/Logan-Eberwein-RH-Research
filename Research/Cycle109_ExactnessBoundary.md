# Cycle 109 — Exactness Boundary

## What is proved at finite level

Under the simple-even hypothesis for the truncated Weil form, the shifted
rank-one scaling operator is self-adjoint in the ground-state-shifted Weil
quotient. Its regularized determinant is exactly the explicit prefactor times
the Fourier/Mellin transform of the selected finite ground state.

Thus the finite operator's spectrum is exactly the real zero set of that
finite transform.

## What exact zeta identification would require

The desired statement is local-uniform convergence, in one common variable
and normalization,

`F_(lambda,N)(z) -> Xi(z)`.

Since every finite `F_(lambda,N)` has only real zeros, Hurwitz's theorem would
then imply that every zero of `Xi` is real, which is RH.

Therefore proving exact convergence is not a routine numerical extension. It
is an RH-strength theorem. A finite computation, however large, cannot certify
it; it can only provide evidence for the hypotheses of the theorem.

## Current conclusion

The exact finite connection is established. The exact infinite connection is
not established in the repository or in the cited construction. The missing
proof is the common-space, weighted transform convergence of the normalized
finite Weil ground states to the Xi-state.
