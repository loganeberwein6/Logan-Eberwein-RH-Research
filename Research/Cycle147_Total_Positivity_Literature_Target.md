# Cycle 147 — Total-Positivity Target from Literature

The total-positivity audit points to the correct stronger target. Dimitrov and
Xu, *Wronskians of Fourier and Laplace Transforms* (arXiv:1606.05011), give
necessary-and-sufficient Laguerre--Pólya criteria for Fourier transforms of
positive kernels in terms of density properties of an associated correlation
kernel. Their consequence is an equivalent RH condition, not a completed RH
proof.

Thus the next mathematical target is not merely checking positive minors of
the theta kernel. It is proving the required density/total-positivity property
for the specific Riemann theta correlation kernel. This is stronger than
ordinary positive definiteness and exactly matches the failure exposed by
`2 + cos(z)`.

The local finite-minor numerical probe was not used because the computational
workers did not return reliable output.
