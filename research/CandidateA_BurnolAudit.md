# Cycle 11: Rees--von-Mangoldt explicit-formula Gram candidate

## Construction

For `m,n` in `{2,...,X-1}`, define

\[
 T_X(s)_{m,n}=R_X(m,n)\Lambda(m)\Lambda(n)(mn)^{-s/2}/\psi(X),
\]

where `R_X(m,n)=-1` for `mn<X`, `+1` otherwise, and
`psi(X)=sum_{2<=n<X} Lambda(n)`.  This is the proposed Rees--Lambda Gram
candidate from the explicit-formula route.

## Exact structural failure

The Rees factor is an indefinite sign form. Multiplication on both sides by
the diagonal matrix with entries `Lambda(n)n^(-s/2)` is a congruence, so it
does not turn the underlying indefinite form into a positive Gram form. The
negative direction therefore survives every nonzero diagonal weighting.

## Verdict

**DEAD.**  Explicitly inserting `Lambda` fixes an arithmetic label but cannot
simultaneously create positivity or the zeta determinant. The normalization
also drives the tested determinants close to 1 rather than toward a
nontrivial zero set.
